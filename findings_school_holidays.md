# School holidays and the weekday slump

_Generated by `src/04_school_holidays.py`. Revenue indexed to a term-time weekday = 100._

## Does the weekday slump survive the school holidays?

- **Weekday, term time**: 1.9 bookings/day, revenue index 100 (134 days observed)
- **Weekday, school holiday**: 6.1 bookings/day, revenue index 283 (65 days observed)
- **Weekend, term time**: 7.9 bookings/day, revenue index 565 (153 days observed)
- **Weekend, school holiday**: 7.4 bookings/day, revenue index 407 (36 days observed)

**Weekday demand rises 3.21x in bookings and 2.83x in revenue during school holidays**, with no change to pricing, marketing or opening hours.

The weekend-to-weekday revenue gap narrows from **5.65x in term time** to **1.44x during school holidays**.
That closes roughly **91%** of the gap.

### Party bookings specifically

- Weekend, term time: 1.06 party bookings/day
- Weekday, term time: 0.10 party bookings/day
- Weekend, school holiday: 0.25 party bookings/day
- Weekday, school holiday: 0.11 party bookings/day

Midweek party bookings run **1.1x higher during school holidays**.

### What this means

The midweek gap is substantially structural. Children are in school Monday to Friday in term time, so the family and birthday segment is not free to visit - and the moment that constraint lifts, midweek demand rises on its own without any intervention.

That reframes the recommendation. Broad midweek discounting during term time is pushing against a constraint VRXtra does not control, and would most likely discount customers who were coming anyway. The addressable midweek audiences are the ones **not** governed by the school calendar: corporate team socials and away-days, university groups, and school trips themselves. Family-focused midweek promotion belongs in the holidays, when the audience is actually available.
